01

App and developer

Oynika is a party game app for Android and iOS. Tabu, Sessiz Sinema, Aramızdaki Casus and Ben Kimim? are played together on one device. Oynika is developed by Beril Bölükbaşı, an independent developer. Developer profile Berilblks.

02

Information used on your device

You can play without creating an account or signing in. Player and team names, scores, timers, game state and the theme choice are used in app memory. Gameplay data is not sent to a developer server. The reviewed version has no separate analytics or crash reporting SDK.

If you choose tilt controls in Ben Kimim?, the app uses accelerometer and gyroscope readings on your device to recognise the motion. The game code does not save or transmit the raw readings. Touch controls are also available. The core games work offline.

03

AdMob and advertising technology

Oynika uses Google AdMob for ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. These services can connect to Google over the internet. Depending on the platform, device settings and ad configuration, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can process the following information and share it with relevant advertising parties:

  • Your IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Advertising identifiers, app-scoped device identifiers and other platform-provided identifiers, where available.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, video interactions, app interactions and performance or error diagnostics.

Google and ad providers may use this information to serve and measure ads, analyse performance, protect security and prevent fraud. Advertising technology can use device identifiers and local storage. The developer does not use these technologies to build a separate player profile.

04

Consent, choices and personalisation

On startup, the app attempts to update its advertising consent status through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). If the relevant message is published in AdMob and UMP indicates that a form is required, the app displays it. On both platforms, the app checks whether UMP allows ad requests before making them. If the update fails, the UMP SDK may allow ad requests based on a valid consent status from a previous session. If the SDK does not allow requests, the app does not send them. The games remain available in either case.

When UMP indicates that privacy options are required, you can review and change your choice under Settings → Ad privacy options (shown in the app as Ayarlar → Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri). UMP allowing ad requests does not mean you have consented to ad personalisation.

iOS: In the current configuration, Oynika requests non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. Oynika’s own code does not access Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) or request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. Google and relevant third parties may still process data for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention when serving non-personalised ads.

Android: Oynika does not explicitly require all ad requests to be non-personalised. The type of ad Google AdMob can serve depends on your UMP choices, region, device settings and AdMob configuration.

06

Retention and deletion

The app does not write gameplay sessions to a developer-operated database. The session in memory ends when the app process closes. Google SDKs may store ad or consent state on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data where the operating system offers that option may remove local app data. This does not delete data in Google’s systems. Google’s retention and deletion options are governed by its own policies.

Your rights and contact

Depending on the rules that apply where you live, you may have rights such as accessing, correcting or deleting personal data and withdrawing consent. Use the app settings described above to manage advertising choices. Google’s privacy tools provide controls for data held by Google.

08

Children’s privacy

Oynika is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. The app does not require registration or ask for a date of birth. For concerns about a child’s data, contact the developer using the support address.
